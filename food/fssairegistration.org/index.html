What is FOSCOS/fssai registration?

In starting fssai registration is known as the Food Licensing and Registration System (FLRS) and After 1st June 2020, It is replaced by the FoSCoS (Food Safety Compliance System). It was mainly launched for the users of Indian Railways and Konkan Railways on the 1st of January 2021.

The major role of the FSSAI is to protect and promote public health by ensuring the quality of the food which requires proper supervision and the regulation to check all standards of food safety. FSSAI also promotes awareness about food ingredients and food standards. The Central Government authorized the FSSAI to offer a food safety license to all Food Business Operators(FBO). All FBOs have to just follow the rules, regulations, and some standards to maintain the quality of food that is prescribed by the FSSAI.

To acquire the FSSAI license and registration an FBO must meet some demands like the production size, management of food business activities, and level of activity. In the FSSAI registration, the FBO will get the 14 digit License number that is printed on the food packaging box.

FSSAI registration provides a food safety certificate trusted by the food authority of India which ensures the safety of the ingredients and products. FSSAI products will ensure that products undergo various levels of quality checking which helps to decrease the case of adulteration, substandard products, and material.

Types of fssai license registration

FSSAI license registration depends on the category, turnover and size of the business.

FSSAI Basic Registration

The FSSAI basic registration is for FBOs who have registered under small-scale industries and startups and have an annual income of less than Rs 12 lakh. Basic registration under FSSAI can be upgraded as per the productivity graph of the FBO.

FSSAI State License

The FSSAI state license is for FBOs who have registered under medium-scale industries and have an annual income of less than Rs 20 crore. The state license can be also implemented as a central license which is dependent upon the performance graph of the productivity..

FSSAI Central License

The FSSAI Central license is for FBOs which has a large business and the annual income of the industry is more than Rs 20 crore.

Eligibility criteria for FOSCOS FSSAI registration

Depending upon the type of FSSAI registration there are different eligibility criteria for FBOs to get registered in FSSAI.

CASE I: For basic FSSAI registration, FBO has to be registered under small industry and annual income must be less than Rs 12 lakh.
CASE II: To get the FSSAI state license industry has to be registered under a medium scale industry and annual income must be less than Rs 20 crore.
CASE III: To get the FSSAI central license industry has to be registered under a large scale industry and the annual income must be more than Rs 20 crore.

Required documents for FOSCOS FSSAI License/registration

Proclamation Form

Applicants should provide the declaration/proclamation form under the name of the owner of the company.

Authority letter

Applicants have to provide the authority letter to ensure the process of registration.

Property Paper

If the owner has property paper issued by the government then the applicant has to provide a copy of property paper.

Rent Agreement Paper

In the case of the rented property, the owner has to provide a copy of the rent agreement(If rented property)

Food Safety Certificate

Applicants have to provide the food safety management system plan or certificate.

Electricity Bill/Water Bill

Applicants have to give the electricity bill/water bill of the (business)working place.

Photo ID Proof

Applicants should provide a copy of valid photo Id proof like - Aadhar card, voter Id card, for the verification process.

Nominee Name

Applicants have to give the name of the nominee to the company along with the board resolution.

General Questions

Can I sell food products without FSSAI?
No, You can’t sell any food products without having FSSAI registration/license as per the restriction of the Food Safety & Standards act 2006. Every food business has its own food license/registration to sell the product which is mentioned in the FSSAI regulations.
How do I know my FSSAI license type?
The FSSAI license type is mentioned at the bottom of the registration form/license certificate provided by the FSSAI.
Is FSSAI registration mandatory?
As per the Food Safety & Standards act 2006, registration in FSSAI is mandatory for all retailers, sellers, restaurants, and for food manufacturing companies.

So, if you want to sell any food product then it must be registered in the FSSAI to improve the quality of the product and to build a better customer relationship
Can we have two FSSAI licenses?
Yes, you can have more than one FSSAI license as a requirement for each product. If the license is taken for processing of milk, fruits & vegetables so the requirement of portion will be different and different licenses will be required

STEP 6: Then after the processing, your FSSAI registration/license number will be sent to your given Email ID.

Can I renew my license?
Yes, Food Business Operators (FBO) can renew their FSSAI license before 30 days of the expiry date of the license. You have to fill the form A or B as per your business type along with your current license number which has to be renewed.
How to check my FSSAI license number online?
To check the FSSAI license number follow the steps below:


STEP 1: Click on the FSSAI registration website.

STEP 2: Then you will be redirected to the FSSAI license number page.

STEP 3: Then type your 14 digit FSSAI registration number. The Wrong FSSAI number will pop up on the screen.

STEP 4: Then the page will redirect to the license profile page, submit your details and get your FSSAI license number online.

What is the validity of the FSSAI license?
The validity of the FSSAI license lies between 1-5 years and the applicant has to apply for the renewal of the license number before 30 days of the expiry of the current license.
