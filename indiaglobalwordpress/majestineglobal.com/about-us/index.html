About Us

Let's Know More About
Majestine Global Exporter

Majestine Global Export is a proud extension of a legacy that began in 1987. What started as a humble wholesale business has now grown into a dynamic export house, carrying forward decades of experience, trust, and excellence in trade. Our parent company has served the Indian market with integrity and consistency for over three decades, and with the inception of Majestine Global Export, we are taking this legacy global.

Based in the heart of Khari Baoli, Chandni Chowk – Asia’s largest spice market, we are deeply rooted in India’s rich trading heritage. This historic market, which dates back to the 17th century, has been a hub of spice trade and commerce for centuries. Bustling with vibrant energy and aromas, Khari Baoli is not only a marketplace but a symbol of India’s diverse flavors and traditional business practices. Our location in this iconic trading district allows us direct access to the freshest, highest quality products — right from the source.
Majestine Global Export specializes in a diverse portfolio including premium Indian Spices, Fresh Fruits, Millets, Fresh Vegetables, Copper Articles, Hardware Products, and Traditional Indian Beds (Khatiya). We work closely with trusted farmers, skilled artisans, and certified manufacturers to ensure that every product we export meets the highest standards of authenticity, freshness, and durability.
Our mission is simple — to bring the richness of Indian tradition and taste to the global market, while offering personalized service, competitive pricing, and timely delivery. We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients, driven by our core values of transparency, quality, and customer satisfaction.
Whether you’re looking for the bold taste of handpicked Indian spices, the health benefits of ancient grains like millets, or the timeless charm of handcrafted Indian khatiyas and copperware, Majestine Global Export is your trusted global sourcing partner.

What We Believe

Our Mission

At Majestine Global Exporter, our mission is to deliver premium-quality Indian products to global markets. We strive to promote India’s rich agricultural and cultural heritage through our exports. Customer satisfaction, timely delivery, and uncompromised quality are at the heart of our operations.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized and trusted name in the export industry. We aim to expand our presence by offering a diverse range of authentic Indian products. Innovation, excellence, and sustainability guide our growth journey. Our vision is to connect the world with the essence of India.

Our Value

Integrity, quality, and reliability form the foundation of our business. We value transparency in every transaction and relationship. Commitment to customer satisfaction drives our continuous improvement. Respect for culture, people, and the environment shapes our decisions.
