Warli Painting – Maharashtra

Enquiry On WhatsApp

Specifications: Natural dyes on cow dung-treated canvas or paper; monochromatic (usually white on brown).

Size: Customizable; typically 12”x18” to 36”x48”.

History: Originating from the Warli tribe of Maharashtra, this ancient tribal art dates back to 2500 BCE. Created mostly by tribal women, it depicts daily village life, animals, and rituals in geometric forms. A beautiful balance between folk storytelling and minimalism.
